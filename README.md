# Pixel Galaxy

**[▶ Play it now](https://jaydenstockalerts-cmyk.github.io/pixel-galaxy/)** — free, no account, no ads, no install. Your empire saves in your browser.

A retro pixel space empire builder. Build up your home station, train a fleet, and conquer systems all the way to the Dark Nexus, the big boss at the far edge of the galaxy.

## How to play

- **Build** — place Solar Arrays, Metal Foundries, Crystal Mines, Trade Hubs and Hangars on your station's plots. Plots unlock as your Command Station levels up.
- **Train a fleet** — build Fighters, Cruisers and Capital Ships in the Hangar. Every enemy fleet has a weakness: swarm fleets fall to Fighters, armored to Cruisers, fortresses to Capital Ships.
- **Conquer** — pick a system, check the odds, choose a stance (All-out, Balanced, Cautious), and send your fleet. Travel takes time, and the battle resolves when your fleet arrives.
- **Defend** — rival factions expand, raid your systems, and can be bought off with tribute or faced down with a garrison. Reinforce a system while a raid warning counts down.
- **Win** — hold 13 systems, or conquer the Dark Nexus.

## Features

- 32-system galaxy in two sectors, 5 rival factions with diplomacy (tribute, pacts, alliances)
- Guided 17-step tutorial, quests, colonies, crew and commanders, research tree
- Offline progress — your empire keeps working while you're away
- Save export/import — back up your empire as text and restore it in any browser
- Free to play, no account, no ads

## Run it yourself

No build step, no dependencies. Clone the repo and open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server:

```
git clone https://github.com/jaydenstockalerts-cmyk/pixel-galaxy.git
cd pixel-galaxy
python3 -m http.server
```
